There have been multiple times in my life where I told myself that I wanted to have a website. However, I've been procrastinating on this since my freshman year of high school when I was first learning Javascript (oh if you could see me now Mr. Carr :car:). Even so, the fact that I'm finally working an internship means that I have to keep myself sane with passion projects because I don't think I'm built for the 40 hours a week grind. Plus, there are so many things to do in the Boston area/through MIT that I don't see why I should let myself sit still. It's unreasonable that I attend M-I-fuggin-T and don't throw myself into random sidequests they offer.

In fact, I signed up for a bunch of makerspace trainings and a few parallel programming classes next week. I'm excited for the parallel programming ones since I suspect there are some applications to astrodynamics I could come up with. It'd be sick if I could bother Kerri about doing a UROP in something like that. Astrodynamics was far and away my favorite class last semester and daddy wants to do more stuff with it :weary:.

I'm slowly but surely getting back into shape. It's nice that Will's my roommate because holy moly that man is fit, and more than that, he's disciplined. Hella disciplined. So disciplined, in fact, that I just have to ask when he's going to the gym to workout, run, or swim and I just follow him there. It's the exact same way I stayed on top of waking up at 5 a.m. every day back when I would do summer xc training in high school: count on other people being there to workout and use them as motivation to go. I'm basically peer pressuring myself to be healthy, but it's working:bangbang: 

I went on a swim a few days ago and it was honest-to-goodness the first one I've had since high school where I felt like I could grab onto a slow pace and hold it forever. A few days after that I even did a full blown workout with Will. He's much more muscular and fit than I am, but it edifies my ego whenever we'd do reps involving breaststroke because I mog the hell out of him with those. I remember for one rep we went out of order, and with a 5-second deficit, I still caught and passed him. I may be significantly weaker than Will, but my swimming peak was still greater than his. Furthermore, though my muscular strength isn't what it used to me, my muscle memory persists, and that's what counts in breaststroke, where you don't have the luxury of continuous propulsion.

Part of why I made this post was to have something when I pushed overhaul part 2 to my repo for this site. Even so, I think I could get used to using this as a journal on top of documenting interesting things in my life.
