## Sick Workout

I do want to preface this by saying I lowkey experienced random pains in the "easy" run I did shortly after this one... but I digress this has been the best workout I've done in ages. The workout was 4x1600 meters (in my mind 4x1 miles), when I originally planned to do just 3. With the watch having arrived, I was excited to try out a track interval workout and see what things felt like. My goal pace for the half marathon is 7:00 / mile, so I targeted 6:30 as a way of getting myself used to going faster than that pace so 7:00 feels easy. The warmup mile felt a little sus but I figured I'd be fine after doing my dynamics and a couple of strides.

I will let the numbers speak for themselves. 
![workout splits](/assets/blog/630_splits.png)

The actual splits are the middle column, and the extrapolated mile pace (i.e. Coros adding 9 extra meters to my run) is to the right of that. I'm only a little miffed that my thingy says I ran 0.99 mile repeats. Who knows, maybe I'll use this as an excuse to do the full mile repeats from now on haha. Regardless, this workout went really really well. I think it's clear I've a long way to go given how the breaks I took just kept getting longer throughout the reps, but the fact that I never dipped above 6:30 for the reps feels pretty dang good.

As an aside, the buttons on the watch continue to be a saving grace for me. Goodness gracious I love me some physical buttons. I feel like a dang olympian even though I'm running at the pace they relax in xD.

## It's Getting More Real

The thoughts below are more sporadic, and that's largely because it's late and I want to get some other thoughts on what's going on in my life written before bed. These posts are little snapshots of my life and I want them to be somewhat substantive and revealing about where I'm at for myself or other people to read down the line. If you're reading this while I'm dead, that's a pretty good sign that the world ain't doing so bad. Please don't tell me the doom-n-gloom YouTube video essays were onto something in any capacity. That would be heart breaking.

I have the FPOP starting in a few days and I have yet to finish recreating the Astrodynamics final project. I gotta get on that ASAP I fear. I'll hopefully convince myself tomorrow morning to start being productive, but we'll see. Things are picking up more with the satellite team and I'm starting to become more involved. I'm mostly doing stuff as treasurer, but I'm eeking towards the technical work and I realize that I'll need to seriously put some good knowledge under my belt so things don't become another repeat of the last two years I've been on a build team. Dude, if I get crushed by the end of the semester again I'm going to be PISSED. 

Speaking of the FPOP, <a href='https://i.ytimg.com/vi/hUAwg7qp2DM/sddefault.jpg'>she</a> and my advisor are going to be there. In my hubris and incessant need to be constantly participating in things, I signed up to be in everything I could :cry: It's fine, the newbies will learn to either love or despise me. Fingers crossed one of them turns out to be a CEO for some massive company and gets me a big-bucks job :money: I'm not really nervous for it, only thing (maybe) is that I'll be repping satellite team for a presentation near the end of the program, and I don't want these kids to be bored or turned off from the team because I was being a goober earlier in the program. I'm kinda interested to see how it turns out, namely because I didn't attend my year's FPOP (for I foolishly thought it was like Interphase and would've required me to spend the entire summer at MIT. Ultimately still worth it not to have gone since I got that extra time with my friends and family).

I've also been playing around a lot with Git since the astro project tutorials have been kinda messy. The Hohmann Transfer demonstration was lowkenuinely generated by Gemini, but I wrote up all of the analysis myself. However, I realize that I want to write up a smaller number of functions that have reproducible use across all of these tutorials, so I'm refactoring the plotting code into just a few functions. I want to knock out like two tutorials before the semester starts and I think it's possible. However, I want to get those done ASAP as the Astro project I really really want done before I start applying around to internships. If I can get one before the career fair, that'll be perfect.

What's one thing that's got me nervous? Aside from the semester, not much. I just really pray that the lows I get to during the semester aren't as low as last year. Jesus. You know there were multiple times last fall wehre I slept in lounges on campus because I stayed up late and felt I was better off squeezing a few more minutes of crappy sleep than making the trek back to my room across the river? That's the type of thing that would make my classmates look at me different, especially when you consider that the class I did those maneuvers for I'm repeating this fall :skull: I hate hate HATE what this place does to me. Yeah yeah yeah everyone's so smart and apparently everyone's struggling at the time (which is a funny statement coming from the people who tell me not to compare myself to them, despite the otherwise omnipresent insistence that we're all intellectual equals here) and I'm not allowed to be angry at anyone or anything because everything is ultimately my fault. It's crazy how the wins I have are seldom just mine and the losses are almost exclusively mine. That's some bull dude. The first A I'll get through just a solo effort (16.09 as I hear from everyone how understimulating and boring it is, so it'll be manageable for me) is going to be waaayyy too late in my MIT career to really matter (I keep deluding myself into thinking a good grad school is in the cards for me).

I like the friends I have here, and I've been able to do a lot of cool stuff. I'm not going to act like it's been cactus diddling in my rectum from start to finish. That said, I really don't know if I'd go back and do it all again. Maybe I should've gone to Rice. Everyone looks happy at Rice U and maybe I'd escape the awful awful culture here. Looking forward to the Cambridge Half though! I think I can make it happen if I don't injure myself before then.
